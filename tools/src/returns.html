How to return an item

  1. Write to {{email}} with your order number and tell us you would like to return the item.
  2. We reply with the return address and instructions.
  3. Send the organizer back unused and in its original packaging. You pay the return postage, unless the item was faulty or not as described.
  4. We refund the item price to your original payment method within 7 days of receiving the parcel.

Damaged, faulty or wrong item

If your organizer arrives damaged, faulty or is not what you ordered, send us a photo within 14 days of delivery. We replace it or refund you in full, including shipping, and you do not need to send it back.

Cancelling an order

Write to us as soon as possible. If the order has not shipped yet, we cancel it and refund you.
