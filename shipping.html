Shipping

Orders ship from our partner warehouse overseas and arrive in 7 to 12 business days.

Cost

Shipping is free on every order to the United States. The total you see at checkout is the total you pay.

Delivery time

Expect 7 to 12 business days from the day you order. This is an estimate: customs and local couriers can add a few days, especially around public holidays.

Tracking

We email a tracking link as soon as your parcel ships. If the tracking page shows no updates for a few days at the start, that is normal for international parcels. Write to hello@seon-line.example if you have not received a tracking email after 3 working days.

Customs and taxes

We currently ship to addresses in the United States. The price at checkout is final, with no extra fees on delivery.

Wrong address or missing parcel

Check your address carefully before you pay. If a parcel is lost in transit, write to us and we will send a replacement or refund you.
